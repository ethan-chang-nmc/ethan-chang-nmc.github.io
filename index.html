Ethan Chang

Hi! I’m a neuroscience Ph.D. student at Washington University in St. Louis, where I study how astrocytes interact with neural circuits and how those ideas might inform computational models of the brain.

The brain contains so much more than just neurons. Recent literature has pointed to astrocytes as integral parts of controlling certain aspects of circuit function. I aim to take these biological inspirations and integrate them into machine learning and reinforcement learning paradigms. Mainly, I am interested in two questions: (1) Can we use biologically inspired models to drive breakthroughs in machine learning models? (2) Can we use these computational models to better understand computation in vivo, and use them to drive cures for neuro-related diseases?

Research interests

I am broadly interested understanding how intelligence is emergent from neural networks. Lines of research include analyzing artificial neural networks and investigating how neuronal/non-neuronal cell functions shape neural computation. At the moment, I am thinking about astrocytes not just as support cells, but as active participants in the dynamics of neural circuits.

  • Deep learning and reinforcement learning
  • Astrocyte function in neural circuits
